Mountain Escape

Tagaytay

Nestled high on a scenic ridge just hours away from the metro, Tagaytay is the ultimate relaxing getaway famous for its year round cool weather and dramatic vistas. Explore Sky Ranch, Tagaytay Picnic Grove, People's Park in the Sky, or the alpine village of Crosswinds. Warm up with a comforting bowl of authentic local bulalo!

World Heritage

Palawan

Crowned as one of the best islands in the world, Palawan is a true tropical masterpiece filled with dramatic limestone cliffs, lush jungles, and vibrant marine life. Explore Twin Lagoon in Coron, the Subterranean River in Puerto Princesa, or island hop across pristine El Nido shores.

🌡️ Avg: 28°C - 32°C Inquire Trip
Cultural Landmark

Ifugao

Carved entirely by hand into the majestic Cordillera mountains over two millennia ago, the Ifugao Rice Terraces are often called the "Eighth Wonder of the World." Discover Banaue, Batad amphitheater slopes, cascading Tappiyah Falls, and rich indigenous culture.

🌡️ Avg: 18°C - 24°C Inquire Trip
Tropical Playground

Boracay

Famous across the globe for its powdery white sand and stunning turquoise waters, Boracay is the ultimate tropical island getaway. Enjoy White Beach sunsets, cliff dives at Ariel's Point, and world-class windsurfing at Bulabog Beach.

🌡 Avg: 27°C - 31°C Inquire Trip
Natural Wonder

Bohol

Famous worldwide for its over a thousand symmetrical, cone-shaped mounds that turn rich chocolate brown during dry season. Take a floating lunch cruise down Loboc River, meet Philippine Tarsiers, or lounge on Panglao Island's white sands.

🌡️ Avg: 26°C - 30°C Inquire Trip
